# Summer Nights: Itch.io Theme Guide

To perfectly align your itch.io store page with the game's in-engine "Golden UI" design system, apply the following settings in the **Edit theme** sidebar (as shown in your screenshot):

### Colors
*   **BG:** `#05030D` *(This is the ultra-dark purple-black used for the global pause menu)*
*   **BG 2:** `#0D051A` *(This is the slightly lighter dark panel color used for UI containers)*
*   **Text:** `#FFFFFF` *(Pure white for maximum legibility against the dark background)*
*   **Link:** `#FFBF26` *(The signature deep golden yellow used for primary accents)*

### Text
*   **Font:** **Lato** or **Roboto** *(These are the closest matches to the in-game 'Inter' font for clean, modern readability)*
*   **Size:** **Large** *(Keep this on Large to mimic the chunky arcade feel)*

### Layout & Images
*   **Screenshots:** Keep this on **Auto** so they display beautifully on the right side.
*   **Banner/Background:** If you don't have custom banners yet, leaving them blank is perfectly fine since the `#05030D` background is highly immersive on its own.
