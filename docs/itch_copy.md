### 🔥 Cool Down the Sun
**Summer Nights** is a stylized, high-stakes 3D arcade shooter built in Godot 4. Your island is baking, and the sun is furious. Take aim, manage your water reserves, and blast the heat away before the temperature reaches 100%.

### 🌊 Core Mechanics
*   **Shoot the Sun:** Fire your water-based weapons to bring the sun's temperature down.
*   **Dodge & Survive:** Intercept incoming hazards like Solar Flares and physically resist Solar Winds.
*   **Water Management:** Watch your crosshair! If your tank drops below 25%, it will pulse orange. Don't run dry when you need it most!

---

### 🔫 Key Features
*   **5 Unlockable Weapons:** Expand your arsenal with unique guns as you progress. Switch seamlessly using the slow-motion Weapon Wheel.
*   **Power-Ups:** Unleash the targeted Ice Burst or perform the massive Sun Dunk (Catastrom Ultimate) to save a doomed run.
*   **Dynamic Weather:** Adapt to Rainstorms (infinite water) and Solar Eclipses (rapid shadow flares) that radically alter the gameplay.
*   **Endless Mode (Rogue-lite):** Prove your endurance. Once you conquer Normal Mode, dive into Endless Mode where you draft stackable perks (like +15% Water Capacity or +15% Critical Damage) after every Boss Wave.
*   **Meaningful Achievements:** Unlock achievements that grant permanent, active buffs to give you a fighting chance as the waves get harder.
*   **Fully Accessible:** Features a dedicated "Reduce Motion" mode that safely converts intense screen shakes and flashing warnings into smooth, static visuals without losing the stakes.

---

### ⚠️ Hazards & Sun Mechanics
*   **Solar Flares:** Shoot them out of the sky to prevent a massive heat spike. Failing to intercept them results in a heavy screen-shaking impact!
*   **Solar Winds:** Severe gusts that physically push your aim sideways, making it harder to target the sun.
*   **Magma Debris:** Searing rocks that crash onto the beach and persist until you evaporate them.
